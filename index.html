06 / FAQ

Before we begin.

Still have a question? Send me an email.

What kinds of clients do you work with?+

I work with founders, growing businesses, and teams that need a stronger brand, a clearer website, or consistent creative support. Clients can be based anywhere; the process works remotely.

How long does a project take?+

Most focused identity or website projects take two to four weeks. Larger systems may take longer. After a short discovery conversation, you’ll receive a clear scope and timeline before work begins.

What do you need to provide a quote?+

A short overview of your business, what you need, your preferred timeline, and an approximate budget are enough to start. If the brief is still forming, I can help define it.

How are payments and revisions handled?+

Projects begin with a 50% deposit, with the balance due before final delivery. The agreed scope includes two focused revision rounds, so feedback stays clear and the project keeps moving.

Can you support the brand after launch?+

Yes. Ongoing design support is available for teams that need regular campaign, social, print, or website updates after the core project is complete.
